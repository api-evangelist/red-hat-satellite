Organizations operating under strict data governance requirements such as GDPR, DORA, and NIS2 increasingly want AI-powered infrastructure management but cannot send data to the cloud. Red Hat Lightspeed on premise delivers that infrastructure intelligence while keeping data inside the organization's firewall to maintain regulatory compliance.
